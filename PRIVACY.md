# Gentle Focus — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 September 2026_

**Short version: Gentle Focus collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no servers. Everything stays in your browser, on your device.**

## What we collect

Nothing is collected by us, because there is no "us" to collect it — the extension has no backend, no accounts, and no analytics.

The following is stored **locally in your browser** (`chrome.storage.local`) and never leaves your device:

- Your settings (timer length, tone, strictness, and so on)
- Your blocklist (the sites you chose to gate)
- Your usage statistics (gates passed, minutes per site, streaks)

## What we do not do

- We do not transmit any data off your device.
- We do not use servers, accounts, sign-in, or cloud sync — not even Chrome's own cross-device sync.
- We do not use analytics, telemetry, tracking, cookies, or advertising.
- We do not sell or share data with anyone.

## About the sites you visit

To decide whether to show a gate, the extension checks the URL of the page you are opening against the blocklist you configured. This matching happens entirely on your device, in the moment. Those URLs are **not** logged, stored, or sent anywhere. Page contents are never read.

## Your data is yours

You can export all of your data or erase it completely at any time from the extension's Settings page. Removing the extension deletes its local storage.

## Permissions

Gentle Focus requests only the browser permissions it needs to gate sites locally (host access to match your blocklist, navigation and tab access to redirect to the gate, storage for your settings, alarms and notifications for timers and session ends, and scripting for the "time almost up" reminder). None of these are used to collect or transmit data. Each is explained in the store listing.
